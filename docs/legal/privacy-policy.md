## 1. The short version

- We collect what we need to run the app: your account, your runs (as distances and times, not routes), your rewards and, if you connect it, your Strava activities.
- Your location is used **on your phone** to measure runs and to sort cafés by distance. Your route and your position are not sent to our servers.
- We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.
- You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time: **Profile → Delete account**.

## 2. What we collect and why

| Information | What it is | Why we use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Account** | Email address and password. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form; we never see it. | To create your account and sign you in. |
| **Profile** | Display name and, if you add one, a profile photo. Photos are stored privately and are only visible to you. | To personalise the app. |
| **In-app runs** | While a run is being tracked, your phone uses GPS (including in the background, while your screen is locked) to measure distance and pace. When a run passes 5 km, we receive **the distance and the start time** of that run. | To unlock rewards for qualifying runs and check they are plausible. |
| **Strava activities** (only if you connect Strava) | With your permission ("view data about your activities"), for each activity: its Strava ID, your Strava athlete ID, the activity name, sport type, distance, moving and elapsed time, start date and whether it was entered manually. We also store the access tokens Strava gives us, **encrypted**. | To find qualifying runs and walks of 5 km or more and unlock rewards automatically. |
| **Rewards** | When each reward was unlocked, which run or activity unlocked it, which café you chose and when you used it. | To give you rewards, enforce the reward rules and show your history. |
| **Notifications** (only if you allow them) | A push notification token for your device and its platform (iOS or Android). | To tell you when a reward unlocks, when it is about to expire, or if Strava gets disconnected. |
| **Technical logs** | Our hosting provider records technical information such as IP addresses and request times. | Security, preventing abuse and fixing problems. |

**Location for nearby cafés.** If you allow location access, your phone compares your position with café locations to show distances and sort cafés nearest first. This happens on your phone; your position is not sent to us. To find a café on the map, your phone may look up the café's address using Apple's or Google's mapping service.

**Stored on your phone.** The app keeps some information on your device so it works quickly and offline: a copy of your rewards, profile, the café list and app settings. Deleting the app removes it.

We do not collect special personal information (such as health information) and we do not knowingly collect information from children (see section 9).

## 3. Who we share it with

We only share what each service needs to work, and they process it on our behalf or under their own terms:

| Service | What it receives | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (database, authentication, file storage and server functions) | Everything listed in section 2 that is stored on our servers | European Union (Ireland) |
| **Strava** (only if you connect it) | Requests to read your activities and, when you disconnect or delete your account, a request to withdraw our access | United States |
| **Expo** (push notification delivery) | Your device's push token and the content of the notification | United States |
| **Apple / Google** | Push notification delivery to your device; map and address look-ups made by your phone | Various |

**Cafés and run clubs** do not receive your personal information from us. When you use a reward, staff see only what is on your reward pass screen.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our users, the public or our rights.

**Outside South Africa.** Some of these services store or process information outside South Africa. We only use providers that are bound by data-protection laws or agreements that give your information a level of protection comparable to POPIA.

## 4. Strava

If you connect Strava, we only read your activities; we never post, change or delete anything on Strava. Information from Strava is shown only to you. RUN is not affiliated with or endorsed by Strava.

**If you disconnect Strava,** we withdraw our access at Strava, delete your Strava connection and tokens, and erase the activity names, sport types and times. We keep each activity's ID, your Strava athlete ID, and each activity's date, distance and reward decision, so that the same activity can never unlock a second reward. These remaining records are deleted when you delete your account.

You can also withdraw access from Strava's side at any time (Strava → Settings → My Apps).

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Account, profile, rewards and Strava records:** until you delete your account.
- **Profile photos:** replaced photos are deleted straight away; the current photo is deleted with your account.
- **Push tokens:** removed when you log out, when your device stops accepting notifications, or when you delete your account.
- **Strava sign-in links:** expire after 10 minutes.
- **Backups and logs:** may keep deleted information for a short period before it is overwritten.

## 6. Deleting your account

Go to **Profile → Delete account** and type DELETE to confirm. We then permanently delete your account, profile and photo, rewards and history, Strava connection and activity records, and notification tokens, and we withdraw our access at Strava. This cannot be undone. Any unused reward is lost.

## 7. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

- ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
- ask us to correct or delete it (you can edit your name and photo, and delete your account, in the app);
- object to how we process it, or withdraw consent you have given (for example by turning off location or notifications, or disconnecting Strava);
- complain to the **Information Regulator (South Africa)**: [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za).

To make a request, email **[CONTACT EMAIL]**. We may need to confirm your identity first.

## 8. Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Strava tokens are encrypted before they are stored. Access to your data on our servers is restricted to your own account. Profile photos are stored privately. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

## 9. Age

RUN is for people aged **18 and older**. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
